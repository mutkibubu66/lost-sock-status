One status update of at most 120 characters, in the voice of a lost sock, for the [Funniest Lost Sock Status Update](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce) bounty on make.fun — an AI judge picks the funniest head to head, winner takes $2 in USDC.

## The line

> Day 47 in the glovebox. The car has no feet, no laundry, and no plan to return me. I have started a union of one.

## Why it works

The premise is clear and fresh: the sock is not lost in a dryer or missing its pair, it is filed away in a car's glovebox for 47 days — somewhere a sock should never be. The status-update frame ("Day 47") makes it a real update, the weary voice sounds like a sock that has given up waiting, and the punchline turns the complaint into a tiny act of defiance: a union of one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (113 characters, no trailing newline).
